Build a real-time night scene that runs in the browser: the courtyard of a post-Soviet housing estate in late November. It only needs to feel right and let me stand at a window or fly a free camera through it.

PRIORITIES (in this order when they conflict)
1. Mood: dark, lonely, a little uncanny
2. Detail that holds up when the camera flies close
3. Stable frame pacing
4. Small bundle size

CONSTRAINTS
- No Three.js/Babylon.js or other engines. WebGPU rendered.
- Everything procedural: no texture/model/sound files. Deliverable is a single self-contained index.html. Report minified size.
- Zero per-frame JS allocations (no GC stutter). Preallocate all buffers.

SCENE
- A yard between brick five-storey houses and panel nine-storey towers, laid out after the reference photos in assets/
- Parked cars, a playground (slide, swings, sandbox under a tin toadstool), benches, bins, garages, a transformer booth
- Cold white street lamps with halos and cones in the haze, hard shadows, one lamp failing; warm bulbs over the doors
- Light snow falling, a thin patchy cover on the lawns and the car roofs, wet asphalt with puddles
- Wind in gusts: bare trees, wires, a swing, weeds and scraps of paper on the asphalt move with it
- Few lit windows, each with a different room behind it: wallpaper, carpets, wall units, curtains, lamps, televisions
- Far away: now and then a car on the road, and a night train that passes with its row of lit windows
- A radio mast and a boiler-house chimney with red beacons, steam

CONTROLS
Start in a dark room at the window. WASD move, mouse look (pointer lock), Q/E down/up, Shift to hurry, 1-6 viewpoints. The window can be flown out of.

SOUND
Synthesised only: wind, the hum of the town, far cars, the train, the buzz of the failing lamp, the creak of the swing; muffled behind the glass, with a clock ticking in the room.

VERIFICATION
- Renders from the six fixed viewpoints compared with the reference photos; close-ups of windows, cars and the ground.
- The real page driven as a visitor would: menu, start, keys, all three detail levels, no console errors.
- Performance: GPU timestamp queries plus a frame-time log from the scripted 60 s fly-through at 2560x1440.
